<p class="research-intro">PhD researcher in machine learning and molecular dynamics.</p>

I am a doctoral researcher in applied mathematics at École des Ponts, advised by Tony Lelièvre and Gabriel Stoltz. I am part of CERMICS and the Inria MATHERIALS team.

My research lies at the intersection of machine learning and the physical sciences. I currently work on learning low-dimensional representations of molecular dynamics, with a particular focus on spectral representation for enhanced sampling. More broadly, I am interested in representation learning for physical systems, multiscale methods, and machine learning for atomistic simulation and materials science.

## About

Before my PhD, I studied applied mathematics at École Polytechnique and in the MVA master's programme, and completed an MBA at the Collège des Ingénieurs. I also completed various research and professional internships alongside my studies.

My PhD is funded by the French Ministry of Ecological Transition.
