About

Scientific profile

My work sits at the intersection of applied mathematics, physics and machine learning. I enjoy designing algorithms that remain faithful to physical principles while leveraging the expressive power of modern neural architectures.

During my PhD I develop models that are both predictive and interpretable, in close collaboration with domain experts. Before that, I trained as an engineer with a strong background in numerical methods, statistics and scientific computing.

Research vision

I am particularly interested in using data-driven models to accelerate simulations, explore complex design spaces and support decisions in energy and climate-related applications.
